### 🩺 Slide 2: The Challenge in Medical Imaging
**Visual:** (MRI scan background, warning/alert icon)

- Medical images are complex, high-dimensional, and often ambiguous.
- Traditional analysis methods can miss subtle patterns.
- Need for advanced, accessible tools to unlock deeper insights.

---

### 🧩 Slide 3: Why Fractals?
**Visual:** (Fractal overlay on anatomy, zoom-in effect)

- Fractals describe self-similar, complex structures found in nature and biology.
- Many anatomical features (vessels, tumors, tissues) exhibit fractal properties.
- Fractal analysis quantifies complexity, aiding diagnosis and research.

---

### 🌟 Slide 4: Project Vision
**Visual:** (Lightbulb, open-source badge, connected nodes)

- Democratize fractal analysis for medical imaging.
- Integrate deep learning for tumor detection.
- Provide a unified, modern, and extensible toolkit for researchers and clinicians.

### 🛠️ Slide 5: Key Features Overview
**Visual:** (Feature icons in grid: fractal, AI, compare, UI)

- Fractal generation (Mandelbrot, Julia, Sierpinski, Newton, etc.)
- Box counting for fractal dimension
- Tumor detection with deep learning
- Image comparison (visual & quantitative)
- Modern PyQt6 UI

### 🎨 Slide 6: Fractal Generation Module

### 📏 Slide 7: Box Counting & Fractal Dimension
**Visual:** (Grid overlay on image, log-log plot)

- Exportable results for research

### 🧠 Slide 8: Tumor Detection Pipeline
**Visual:** (Brain MRI with bounding boxes, ONNX/AI icon)

- Deep learning models (PyTorch/ONNX)
- Pre-trained on axial, coronal, sagittal MRI slices
- Simple workflow: load image, run detection, visualize results
- Output: tumor masks, confidence scores
