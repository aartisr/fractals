# Fractals & Medical Imaging: Project Story (5 Slides)

---

## Slide 1: Transforming Medical Imaging with Fractals

- Medical images are complex—fractal geometry reveals hidden patterns.
- This repo delivers a modern, open-source toolkit for:
  - Fractal generation & analysis
  - Tumor detection with deep learning
  - Visual & quantitative image comparison
- Empowering researchers, clinicians, and students with accessible, robust tools.

---

## Slide 2: All-in-One Python Toolkit

- **Fractal Explorer**: Mandelbrot, Julia, Sierpinski, Newton, and more
- **Box Counting**: Quantitative fractal dimension for any image or ROI
- **Tumor Detection**: Pre-trained deep learning models for brain MRI
- **Image Compare**: Side-by-side, stepwise, and statistical comparison
- **Modern UI**: PyQt6, drag-and-drop, dark mode, accessibility

---

## Slide 3: Architecture & User Experience

- Modular codebase: `fractals/`, `boxcounting/`, `tumors/`, `ui.py`
- Intuitive interface: Tabs for each workflow, live feedback, tooltips
- Drag-and-drop image loading, real-time results, and exportable outputs
- Extensible: Add new fractals, models, or analysis methods easily

---

## Slide 4: Real Impact for Research & Medicine

- Analyze and compare medical images with scientific rigor
- Visualize and quantify tumor regions in MRI scans
- Benchmark algorithms, support clinical QA, and enable new discoveries
- Open source: Welcoming contributions, detailed docs, and active community
