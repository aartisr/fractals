

# Fractals & Medical Imaging: Project Story (5 Slides)

### 🩺 Slide 1: Revolutionizing Medical Imaging with Fractals

**Visual:** *(Background: stylized brain MRI with fractal overlay)*

- Medical images are inherently complex—fractal geometry uncovers patterns invisible to the naked eye.
- This open-source toolkit empowers:
    - Fractal generation and analysis
    - AI-driven tumor detection
    - Visual and quantitative image comparison
- Designed for researchers, clinicians, and students seeking robust, accessible tools.

---



---
### 🛠️ Slide 2: Unified Python Toolkit for Discovery

**Visual:** *(Icons for fractal, AI, comparison, and UI; grid layout)*

- **Fractal Explorer**: Generate and visualize Mandelbrot, Julia, Sierpinski, Newton, and more.
- **Box Counting**: Compute fractal dimensions for any image or region of interest.
- **AI Tumor Detection**: Pre-trained ONNX models for brain MRI analysis.
- **Image Comparison**: Side-by-side, stepwise, and statistical workflows.
- **Modern UI**: PyQt6, drag-and-drop, dark mode, and accessibility features.
